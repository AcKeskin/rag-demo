## Procedural Planets

A real-time computer graphics project. Earth and 12 sci-fi planet types generated on the GPU, with terrain, biomes, oceans, a scattering atmosphere and volumetric clouds. You can fly from orbit to the ground with no loading step. Built with C++17, OpenGL and compute shaders. Source: github.com/AcKeskin/procedural-planets.

## Canvas Deck

An Obsidian plugin for slide decks where every slide is an infinite Excalidraw canvas. Has saved views, a pen, a laser pointer, and PDF and HTML export. Built with TypeScript. Source: github.com/AcKeskin/canvas-deck.

## contexture

Ali's setup for working with AI in Claude Code: coding rules that load before related code, a review pass after, safety hooks and memory. Built with Node.js. Source: github.com/AcKeskin/contexture.

## Last Fighter

A space shooter in Unity (C#) where you defend your station against waves of enemies that keep getting harder. Source: github.com/AcKeskin/LastFighter.

## Virtuanance

Remote AR/VR maintenance, his senior (graduation) project. An expert sees what the technician sees and draws live annotations. Built with Unity, XR and OpenCV. Source: github.com/Virtuanance.

## Saliency cGAN

Research project: predicting where people look in an image with a conditional GAN, plus person and face detection channels. Written in Python.

## rag-demo

This assistant. Retrieval-augmented generation over Ali's CV and projects, running on Cloudflare Workers with hybrid keyword and vector search. Source: github.com/AcKeskin/rag-demo.
